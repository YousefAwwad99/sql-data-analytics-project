## 🚀 Project Overview

This project analyzes **customers, products, and sales data** to identify
important business metrics, customer segments, and product performance.

### 📌 Key Analysis

- 👥 Customer Analysis
- 🛍️ Product Analysis
- 💰 Sales & Revenue Analysis
- 🏆 Top & Bottom Performing Products
- 🎯 Customer Segmentation
- 📈 Business KPIs & Reports
